Service & Maintenance

Instrument Servicing & Repair

ARK Surgicals provides complete post-supply maintenance — sharpening, passivation, joint overhaul, and laparoscopic servicing for hospitals and OT departments across Tamil Nadu.

Sharpening & Edge Restoration

Precision re-sharpening of scissors, chisels, rongeurs, bone cutters, and all cutting instruments to factory-edge specification using calibrated grinding equipment.

Joint & Hinge Repair

Replacement of worn screws, box joints, and ratchet mechanisms on forceps, clamps, needle holders, and retractors. Smooth action restored to OEM tolerance.

Passivation & Anti-Corrosion

Full electropolishing and passivation treatment for SS-410 & SS-420 instruments, restoring corrosion resistance and ensuring continued autoclave compatibility.

Jaw & Tip Realignment

Realignment of mismatched jaw tips, bent blades, and misaligned forceps jaws on tissue forceps, artery forceps, bone holders, and laparoscopic instruments.

Laparoscopic Instrument Overhaul

Full disassembly, cleaning, jaw replacement, insulation check, and reassembly of trocars, graspers, scissors, dissectors, and laparoscopic needle holders.

Instrument Set Audit & Inventory

Complete inspection and documentation of hospital instrument sets — identifying worn, damaged, or missing instruments with full replacement recommendations.
